    🔬 Methodology

    Data source: Cambrian Signal (YC formation data, 6,179 companies, version yc-2026-08-14-semantic-v7) cross-referenced with the YC OSS API.

    Preprocessing: Company counts per category per batch, normalized to share of batch total (batch sizes range from 94 to 398). This removes batch-size bias.

    Model: Time series forecasting model. Zero-shot — no fine-tuning. Config: max_context=1024, max_horizon=16, normalize_inputs=True, use_continuous_quantile_head=True, force_flip_invariance=True, infer_is_positive=True.

    Forecast: 1-step and 2-step ahead point forecast + 10 quantile outputs (mean, 10th–90th percentile). Confidence intervals reflect model uncertainty, not historical volatility.

    Limitations: YC batches are discrete events, not regular time series. Batch composition is curated by YC, not market-driven. The model assumes stationarity after normalization; structural breaks (e.g., "AI" splitting into subcategories) may not be captured. 15 data points is short-context; wider confidence intervals should be expected.
